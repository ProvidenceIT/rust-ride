# Complete Audio Engine

Finish the audio engine implementation using rodio for sound effects, audio cues, and background audio during rides. Includes countdown sounds, achievement chimes, and optional workout music playback.

## Rationale
Audio feedback enhances the training experience and provides important cues during intense efforts. A polished audio experience differentiates RustRide as a complete training solution.

## User Stories
- As a cyclist, I want audio countdown before intervals so that I can prepare for effort changes
- As a user, I want sound effects for achievements so that my accomplishments feel rewarding

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Countdown sounds play before interval changes
- [ ] Achievement and milestone chimes work
- [ ] Audio volume controls function correctly
- [ ] Audio engine initializes reliably on all platforms
- [ ] No audio lag or synchronization issues
